The Challenge

How the challenge works

Four simple steps. 60 seconds. Anyone can do it.

Step 01
📱
Open camera

Phone facing you. No filter. No production. Just you.

Step 02
🦋
The gesture

Hands on heart, open outward like wings. Hold it.

Step 03
💬
Say it

"I got you, [name]." Or: "I see you." Whatever is real.

Step 04
🔗
Tag 3

Nominate 3 people. They have 24 hours. Pass it forward.
